## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet a user story; `/gsd-mvp-phase 2` can convert it): *An existing user's selected video theme plays behind the desktop icons on every monitor on Windows through libmpv, survives shell and monitor changes, and pauses for fullscreen apps.*

This plan's slice: **a renderer surface sits behind the icons on the primary monitor** — the path from the legacy selection through the service gate, the IPC Assign and the renderer's DPI-aware desktop surface, on the real Windows 11 26300 desktop. The desktop attach is the riskiest part of the phase (RESEARCH Summary, Pitfall 1), so it is proven on its own before plan 07 plays video on it.

<objective>
Wire the surface half of the first wallpaper end to end: service gate (Video type, safe relative Source) → spawn one renderer for the primary monitor → `ToRenderer::Assign` → renderer makes itself Per-Monitor-V2 aware and attaches a surface behind the icons (24H2 raised-desktop aware) on a thread that pumps messages. Then turn the attach logic into a pure, fixture-tested classifier with rect math tests.

Purpose: DESK-01 and DESK-06 for the surface; plan 07 (libmpv playback) and everything later (tray, settings, monitors, sync, fullscreen) build on it.
Output: `sucrose-platform` surface, monitors, DPI awareness, event loop and layout; `ToRenderer::Assign`; renderer and service wiring.
</objective>
