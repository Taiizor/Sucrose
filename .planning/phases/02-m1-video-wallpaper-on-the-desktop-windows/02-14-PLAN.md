## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet a user story; `/gsd-mvp-phase 2` can convert it): *An existing user's selected video theme plays behind the desktop icons on every monitor on Windows through libmpv, survives shell and monitor changes, and pauses for fullscreen apps.*

This plan's slice: **every monitor shows the same frame and you hear it once** — the D-02 master clock, follower correction and audio ownership.

<objective>
Add the master/follower roles: the master renderer publishes its playback clock over IPC, the service relays it and elects or promotes the master, and followers correct drift with a pure, unit-tested controller; only the master plays audio.

Purpose: D-02 (the maintainer's "no drift or echo" requirement) on top of plan 12's one renderer per monitor.
Output: `sucrose-engine-mpv/src/sync.rs`, protocol additions, `monotonic_ns` in sucrose-platform, service election.
</objective>
