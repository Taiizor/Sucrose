## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal: *An existing user's selected video theme plays behind the desktop icons on every monitor on Windows through libmpv, survives shell and monitor changes, and pauses for fullscreen apps.*

This plan's slice (UAT gap G-02-7, renderer side, check): **every placement of a surface is read back and judged against its target and the shell's desktop window, and a surface that ended up outside that window says so in the log.**

<objective>
Make every placement of a desktop surface checked: `set_rect` reads the result back and judges it against the target and the parent's client area (a pure `placement_verdict` with unit tests and a real-window test), the attach reports the same verdict, and the renderer logs the parent origin, the local rectangle and the verdict, with a warning when the target lies partly or wholly outside the parent.

Purpose: in UAT test 7(c) the real screen showed the static Windows wallpaper for 91 s after "Make this my main display". Root cause (`.planning/debug/real-screen-static-wallpaper-after-primary-switch.md`): a surface is a child of Progman and is placed in Progman's client coordinates at one instant; in layout C explorer kept Progman at layout B's rectangle [-1920,0,1920,1080] (inferred), so DISPLAY1 [1920,0,3840,1080] became client x = 3840, wholly outside Progman, and was clipped to nothing while the renderer logged "surface moved"; `set_rect` verifies nothing, nothing watches Progman's location, and only monitors whose own rectangle changed are re-placed. This plan makes the state visible (verify and report); 02-24 makes the surface follow its parent; 02-25 re-places every surface after every display change.
Output: `Placement`/`PlacementVerdict`/`placement_verdict`/`RectPx::intersection` in geometry.rs, a checked `set_rect` and attach verdict in surface.rs, renderer logs.

Gap plan set for G-02-7: 02-23 (this plan: every placement is checked and logged), 02-24 (the surface follows explorer's desktop window through an event-driven location watch; FEATURES note), 02-25 (the service re-places every surface after every applied display change, legacy parity), 02-26 (the maintainer runs the instrumented build through the UAT scenario and an origin-only change, then decides what a monitor that explorer leaves outside its desktop window shows). The behavior chosen at the 02-26 checkpoint is planned after that decision (`/gsd-plan-phase 2 --gaps`, reading 02-26-SUMMARY.md), because its tasks and checks depend on the choice and on the run's evidence.

Gap status: this plan contributes to G-02-7 and does not close it. Its frontmatter lists no gap_ids, because verify-work's reconcile_gaps marks a gap resolved as soon as an executed plan that lists it has a SUMMARY; G-02-7 stays open in 02-UAT.md until the plan written after the 02-26 decision makes its truth hold ('fallback'), or is recorded as an accepted gap with the maintainer's sign-off ('wait'), in which case its truth stays unmet while explorer leaves a monitor outside its desktop window.
</objective>

## Design notes (read before Task 1)

- Where a surface shows: its screen rectangle is the parent's client origin plus the client offset fixed at the last `set_rect`. A parent that moves later drags every child by the same delta, and a parent that does not cover the target clips the child away. This plan makes both visible (verdict); 02-24 follows the parent (location watch).
- The 02-16 order guard stays exactly as it is (its hook range EVENT_OBJECT_CREATE..EVENT_OBJECT_REORDER, its process-wide filter, its restore budget and re-check timer). It passed UAT (test 7a).
- A Hidden or PartlyHidden verdict never fails the attach: a renderer that refused to attach would end as a failed start (exit 4) and spend its restart budget (3 per 60 s) while explorer is stale. It attaches, warns and keeps playing; after 02-24 the location watch shows the video the moment explorer moves its window over the target.
- What a monitor shows while explorer leaves it outside its desktop window for good is decided in 02-26 with evidence from the desktop; this plan only makes the state visible in the log.
- No standalone Progman probe: after this plan every placement logs the parent's rectangle and the virtual screen, so the maintainer run in 02-26 answers the diagnosis' open point (Progman's rectangle during layout C) with the fix in place.
