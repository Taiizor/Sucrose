## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal: *An existing user's selected video theme plays behind the desktop icons on every monitor on Windows through libmpv, survives shell and monitor changes, and pauses for fullscreen apps.*

This plan's slice (UAT gap G-02-7, renderer side): **a surface notices when it ended up outside the shell's desktop window, says so in the log, and moves back the moment explorer moves that window.**

<objective>
Make every placement of a desktop surface checked and every later move of its parent window followed: `set_rect` reads the result back and judges it against the target and the parent's client area (a pure `placement_verdict` with unit tests and a real-window test), the renderer logs the parent origin, the local rectangle and the verdict, and a WinEvent hook on the parent's location re-places the surface at its stored screen target whenever explorer moves or resizes that window.

Purpose: in UAT test 7(c) the real screen showed the static Windows wallpaper for 91 s after "Make this my main display". Root cause (`.planning/debug/real-screen-static-wallpaper-after-primary-switch.md`): a surface is a child of Progman and is placed in Progman's client coordinates at one instant; in layout C explorer kept Progman at layout B's rectangle [-1920,0,1920,1080] (inferred), so DISPLAY1 [1920,0,3840,1080] became client x = 3840, wholly outside Progman, and was clipped to nothing while the renderer logged "surface moved"; `set_rect` verifies nothing, nothing watches Progman's location, and only monitors whose own rectangle changed are re-placed. This plan fixes the renderer part (verify, report, follow the parent); 02-24 fixes the service part (re-place every surface after every display change).
Output: `Placement`/`PlacementVerdict`/`placement_verdict`/`RectPx::intersection` in geometry.rs, a checked `set_rect`, a location watch in surface.rs, renderer logs, a FEATURES note.

Gap plan set for G-02-7: 02-23 (this plan: the renderer checks and follows its parent), 02-24 (the service re-places every surface after every applied display change, legacy parity), 02-25 (the maintainer runs the instrumented build through the UAT scenario and an origin-only change, then decides what a monitor that explorer leaves outside its desktop window shows). The behavior chosen at the 02-25 checkpoint is planned after that decision (`/gsd-plan-phase 2 --gaps`, reading 02-25-SUMMARY.md), because its tasks and checks depend on the choice and on the run's evidence.

Gap status: this plan contributes to G-02-7 and does not close it. Its frontmatter lists no gap_ids, because verify-work's reconcile_gaps marks a gap resolved as soon as an executed plan that lists it has a SUMMARY; G-02-7 stays open in 02-UAT.md until the plan written after the 02-25 decision makes its truth hold.
</objective>

## Design notes (read before Task 1)

- Where a surface shows: its screen rectangle is the parent's client origin plus the client offset fixed at the last `set_rect`. A parent that moves later drags every child by the same delta, and a parent that does not cover the target clips the child away. Both have to be seen (verdict) and followed (location watch).
- Event-driven only (D-11, project constraint): the parent is followed through a WinEvent hook on EVENT_OBJECT_LOCATIONCHANGE. No timer, no polling loop.
- The 02-16 order guard stays exactly as it is (its hook range EVENT_OBJECT_CREATE..EVENT_OBJECT_REORDER, its process-wide filter, its restore budget and re-check timer). It passed UAT (test 7a); the location watch is a second, separate hook.
- A Hidden or PartlyHidden verdict never fails the attach: a renderer that refused to attach would end as a failed start (exit 4) and spend its restart budget (3 per 60 s) while explorer is stale. It attaches, warns, keeps playing, and the location watch shows the video the moment explorer moves its window over the target.
- What a monitor shows while explorer leaves it outside its desktop window for good is decided in 02-25 with evidence from the desktop; this plan only makes the state visible in the log and recovers from it when explorer moves.
- No standalone Progman probe: after this plan every placement and every parent move logs the parent's rectangle and the virtual screen, so the maintainer run in 02-25 answers the diagnosis' open point (Progman's rectangle during layout C) with the fix in place.
