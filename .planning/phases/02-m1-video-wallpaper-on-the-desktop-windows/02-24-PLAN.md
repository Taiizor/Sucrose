## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal: *An existing user's selected video theme plays behind the desktop icons on every monitor on Windows through libmpv, survives shell and monitor changes, and pauses for fullscreen apps.*

This plan's slice (UAT gap G-02-7, service side): **after every display change every surface is put back on its monitor, not only the surfaces of monitors whose rectangle changed.**

<objective>
Replace the per-monitor SetRect rule of `Fleet::apply_snapshot` with the legacy rule: after every applied display change, every renderer that stays is told its monitor's rectangle, changed or not, through a pure `topology::replace_all`; spawns and closes stay as they are.

Purpose: a surface's screen position is its parent's client origin plus the offset fixed at its last placement. Today `diff` sends SetRect only to monitors whose rectangle, work area or DPI changed, so when explorer moves Progman for a change that leaves a monitor's rectangle alone (an origin-only change, or a primary switch), that monitor's surface is dragged off it and never told to move (the latent second failure mode named in `.planning/debug/real-screen-static-wallpaper-after-primary-switch.md`). The legacy app re-places every engine window 2000 + 500 ms after each display change (`src/Shared/Engine/Sucrose.Shared.Engine/Event/Handler.cs`, `DisplaySettingsChanged`). Together with the location watch of 02-23 (which follows explorer's moves at any time) this covers both orders of events: explorer moving Progman before or after the service's debounce ends.
Output: `topology::replace_all` with tests, `apply_snapshot` wired to it with Fleet-level tests, a FEATURES row 30 update.
</objective>
