## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal: *An existing user's selected video theme plays behind the desktop icons on every monitor on Windows through libmpv, survives shell and monitor changes, and pauses for fullscreen apps.*

This plan's slice (UAT gap G-02-7, renderer side, follow): **a surface follows explorer's desktop window: the moment explorer moves or resizes that window, the surface is put back on its monitor.**

<objective>
Follow every later move of a surface's parent window: a WinEvent hook on the parent's location re-places the surface at its stored screen target whenever explorer moves or resizes that window, with the checked placement of 02-23; an outside episode is warned once and its end reported; `set_rect` keeps the stored target current; the FEATURES row 'Monitor changes, shell restart recovery (CrashExplorer)' says how it works.

Purpose: a surface's screen position is its parent's client origin plus the offset fixed at its last placement, so a parent that explorer moves after a placement drags the surface off its monitor, and a parent that explorer moves back over a target that lay outside it would show the surface again only if someone re-places it. Root cause and evidence: `.planning/debug/real-screen-static-wallpaper-after-primary-switch.md` (nothing watches Progman's location). 02-23 makes the state visible; this plan follows the parent; 02-25 re-places every surface after every display change.
Output: the location watch in surface.rs with pure decision functions and a real-window test, a note in the FEATURES row 'Monitor changes, shell restart recovery (CrashExplorer)'.

Gap plan set for G-02-7: 02-23 (every placement is checked and logged), 02-24 (this plan: the location watch), 02-25 (the service re-places every surface after every applied display change, legacy parity), 02-26 (maintainer run and decision). The behavior chosen at the 02-26 checkpoint is planned after that decision.

Gap status: this plan contributes to G-02-7 and does not close it. Its frontmatter lists no gap_ids, because verify-work's reconcile_gaps marks a gap resolved as soon as an executed plan that lists it has a SUMMARY; G-02-7 stays open in 02-UAT.md until the plan written after the 02-26 decision makes its truth hold ('fallback'), or is recorded as an accepted gap with the maintainer's sign-off ('wait'), in which case its truth stays unmet while explorer leaves a monitor outside its desktop window.
</objective>

## Design notes (read before Task 1)

- Event-driven only (D-11, project constraint): the parent is followed through a WinEvent hook on EVENT_OBJECT_LOCATIONCHANGE. No timer, no polling loop.
- The 02-16 order guard stays exactly as it is (its hook range EVENT_OBJECT_CREATE..EVENT_OBJECT_REORDER, its process-wide filter, its restore budget and re-check timer, its `GUARDS` registry). It passed UAT (test 7a); the location watch is a second, separate hook with its own registry.
- The watch only ever moves the renderer's own surface; it never moves, resizes, restyles or destroys a shell window.
- Hook delivery cannot be tested in-process (WINEVENT_SKIPOWNPROCESS skips this process's own events); the tests call the watch's re-place function directly, and the maintainer run in 02-26 checks on the desktop that a parent move produces the "desktop window moved" line (class (c) in 02-26 detects a missed move).
- What a monitor shows while explorer leaves it outside its desktop window for good is decided in 02-26; this plan recovers from that state the moment explorer moves its window.
