## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (the Goal line is not in user-story form yet; `/gsd-mvp-phase 2` can convert it, this plan does not invent one): *An existing user's selected video theme plays behind the desktop icons on every monitor on Windows through libmpv, survives shell and monitor changes, and pauses for fullscreen apps.*

This plan's slice: **the libmpv engine binary is delivered and loadable** — a developer runs `cargo xtask fetch-engines` and gets a hash-verified `libmpv-2.dll` next to `sucrose.exe`, and the engine crate loads it through our own bindings. Every later plan that plays video depends on it.

<objective>
Deliver the pinned libmpv build the way D-16/D-028 describe (download, SHA256 check, extract, place next to the dev exe, never committed), commit our own bindgen bindings for the pinned `client.h`, and create `sucrose-engine-mpv` with a loader that follows the D-18 search order and reports D-20 failures as values. Also stop git from ignoring `apps/sucrose/`.

Purpose: libmpv is the engine of ENG-01; the wallpaper tracer (plan 05) needs a DLL to load and bindings to call.
Output: `xtask/engines.toml`, `cargo xtask fetch-engines`, `cargo xtask bindgen-mpv`, `crates/sucrose-engine-mpv` (sys.rs, loader.rs), an ignored load test.
</objective>
