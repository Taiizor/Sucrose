## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet a user story; `/gsd-mvp-phase 2` can convert it): *An existing user's selected video theme plays behind the desktop icons on every monitor on Windows through libmpv, survives shell and monitor changes, and pauses for fullscreen apps.*

This plan's slice: **every monitor, and it keeps working** — one renderer per monitor (D-01), monitor plug/unplug/DPI changes applied as diffs, and recovery after explorer.exe restarts (success criteria 1 and 2).

<objective>
Drive renderers from the monitor topology: a pure diff keyed by device name, debounced display events from the service window, SetRect for geometry/DPI changes, and event-driven shell-restart recovery (TaskbarCreated) with the surface-lost exit exempt from the crash budget.

Purpose: DESK-01 on every monitor, DESK-06 per-monitor DPI after changes, DESK-07 monitor and shell recovery.
Output: `apps/sucrose/src/topology.rs`, platform events and surface changes, `ToRenderer::SetRect`.
</objective>
