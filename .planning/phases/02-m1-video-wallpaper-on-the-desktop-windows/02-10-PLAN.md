## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet a user story; `/gsd-mvp-phase 2` can convert it): *An existing user's selected video theme plays behind the desktop icons on every monitor on Windows through libmpv, survives shell and monitor changes, and pauses for fullscreen apps.*

This plan's slice: **a crashed renderer comes back, but never in a loop, and never outlives the service** — the D-22 restart budget and the job object.

<objective>
Move renderer spawning into a supervisor: every renderer runs in a kill-on-close job object without a console, a waiter thread reports its exit, and a pure restart budget decides between restart-with-backoff and giving up with a visible reason.

Purpose: the crash half of DESK-07 and D-22 ("avoids the legacy restart loops").
Output: `RendererJob` in sucrose-platform, `apps/sucrose/src/supervisor.rs`.
</objective>

On a crash it respawns the same slot after 500 ms and logs `info!(slot, "renderer restarted")`; the new renderer receives the same Assign (the service keeps the last Assignment per slot). The service's `service started` log line gains `pid = std::process::id()` as its first field.
3. `service.rs` uses the Supervisor instead of spawning directly; Quit (plan 09) sends Close through it and kills survivors through their Child handles.
