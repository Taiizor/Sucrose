## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet a user story; `/gsd-mvp-phase 3` can convert it, this plan does not invent one): *The service and the Portal work as one app: the user picks a theme in the Portal's library, "Use" applies it through the service, settings migrate from the C# install and persist, and Sucrose can start with the OS.*

This gap-closure plan's slice: **the source of the paging growth is established by evidence** (SC5, UI-07, 03-VERIFICATION gap 2 "find the growth source: GPUI atlas release, decode buffers, allocator retention"): the D-25 reruns tell the three candidate causes apart, so a pass confirms the atlas cause of 03-27 and a failure names the part that still grows.

<objective>
03-27 fixes the leading cause (images released inside a window update stayed in GPUI's atlas) and proves the mechanism with GPUI tests, but a test cannot show how much of the 500-theme growth (first page 131 MB, peak 287, final 269) the atlas explains, and the 2026-11-24 run recorded no figure that separates the candidates. Paging needs the maintainer's hand (no synthesized input), so the measurement that can separate them is the D-25 reruns themselves. This plan, run before them, makes every rerun carry the diagnosis: the Portal logs its own heap (allocated and committed) and video memory with each page change, and the log-split summary attributes the private-bytes growth to GPU memory (atlas textures), held heap (decode buffers, caches, anything Rust keeps alive) or allocator retention (memory the heap keeps committed after frees), with a mechanical verdict that 03-39 turns into "cause confirmed" or one named follow-up.

Purpose: UI-07 / SC5 gap 2, the diagnosis half of "find and fix the growth source"; the checker's requirement that the plans establish the source by evidence or define the next step when the atlas cause is refuted.
Output: `sucrose_platform::memory_detail`, four new fields on the "page shown" event, `portal_attribution` and its CLI output, tests.
</objective>
